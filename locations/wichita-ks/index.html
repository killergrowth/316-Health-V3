Health Insurance Broker in Wichita, KS

316 Health Insurance has been in downtown Wichita since 2003. Over 20 years, we’ve helped individuals, families, and small businesses across the greater Wichita metro figure out what coverage actually makes sense for their situation — not just what’s available.

We’re independent, which means we compare plans across multiple carriers and make a recommendation based on what’s right for you. A lot of people come in thinking health insurance is just a cost center. Most leave with a plan they actually understand and feel confident about.

Health Insurance Services We Offer in Wichita

Medicare Plans

Medigap Supplements and Part D drug plans for Wichita-area residents turning 65 or reviewing current Medicare coverage.

ACA / Marketplace

Individual and family plans through the Affordable Care Act Marketplace, with subsidy qualification and enrollment support.

Individual & Family

Private health insurance for individuals and families who purchase coverage on their own.

Small Group Plans

Group health insurance for Wichita-area small businesses with under 25 employees.

Self-Employed

Coverage for freelancers, contractors, 1099 workers, and sole proprietors.

COBRA & Turning 26

Guidance for coverage transitions, leaving a job, aging off a parent’s plan, or bridging to Medicare.
