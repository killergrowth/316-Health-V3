A Health Insurance Agency Reimagined to Provide a Better Client Experience

Health insurance can leave people feeling confused, frustrated, and wondering who they can actually trust. We believed there had to be a better way to serve people than simply selling them another insurance product and leaving them alone to figure it out. So we built 316 Health Insurance differently.

We start by putting ourselves in the shoes of the people we serve. What are they trying to fix? What actually matters to them? And if we were sitting in their chair, how would we want someone to help us solve it? Rather than being driven by commissions, we're driven by solving problems. Whether the solution makes us a nickel in commission or not, we're here to give good advice and help people make decisions they understand and feel confident about.

Through our Broker Advocacy Service, we've also built a system designed to walk alongside our clients throughout their health insurance journey. In other words, we don't disappear once the policy is issued. We have an entire team to help clients understand how to use their health insurance tools, advocate alongside them through the claims process, and make sure they have someone in their corner on the day they actually need it.

How we help you find a better way

We put ourselves in your shoes before trying to solve the problem.
We give the same advice whether it makes us a commission or not.
We explain the why, so you understand what you’re choosing—not just what to buy.
When something goes sideways, our advocacy team helps you figure out what happens next.
We stay alongside you after enrollment, because buying insurance is only the beginning.
