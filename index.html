What We Do

We Use Products to Build Strategies.

Our clients turn to us when they are tired of solving the same old problems the same old way. Instead, we help them self-discover a personalized strategy that actually works for them on the day they need it.

Medicare

Medicare Supplements (Medigap) and Part D prescription drug plans for individuals turning 65 or reviewing their current Medicare coverage.

ACA / Marketplace

ACA Marketplace plans for individuals and families who don’t have employer coverage. We help you find subsidies and enroll in the right plan.

Individual & Family

Coverage tailored for individuals and families, including options outside the ACA Marketplace for those who need more flexibility.

Small Business

We help small and medium-sized businesses offer a health insurance package that’s predictable for the employer’s budget and provides real benefit for employees.

Self-Employed

Health coverage for freelancers, contractors, and solo professionals — focused on building a strategy that works when you need it, at a cost you can plan for.

COBRA & Turning 26

Lost employer coverage or aging off a parent’s plan? We walk through your real options so you don’t overpay or end up without coverage.

Christian Sharing Programs

Health Care Sharing Ministries (HCSMs) are faith-based alternatives to traditional insurance where members share each other’s medical costs. Not insurance — but a powerful option for the right household.

Medical Cost Sharing

Medical Cost Sharing (MCS) programs offer an alternative path to managing healthcare expenses — often at a lower monthly cost. We help you understand if it fits your overall strategy.

Direct Primary Care

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a membership model where you pay your doctor directly for unlimited primary care visits. Paired with the right coverage, it can dramatically reduce your overall healthcare costs.
