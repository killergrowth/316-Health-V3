Our Services

What We Do

Most people come to us not because they couldn’t find a plan on their own, but because they didn’t trust that what they were looking at was actually the right decision. That’s where we come in. We’re independent, which means we compare plans across multiple carriers and make a recommendation based on your situation — not on what makes us the most money.

Medicare Plans

Medigap Supplements and Part D drug plans for residents turning 65 or reviewing current Medicare coverage.

Learn More

ACA / Marketplace

Individual and family plans through the Affordable Care Act Marketplace, including subsidy qualification, enrollment, and ongoing support.

Learn More

Individual & Family

Private health insurance for individuals and families who purchase coverage on their own, including plan type comparisons and provider network checks.

Learn More

Small Group Plans

Group health insurance for Wichita-area small businesses with under 25 employees. We compare plans across carriers so you can offer competitive benefits.

Self-Employed

Coverage designed for freelancers, contractors, 1099 workers, and sole proprietors who pay for their own health insurance.

COBRA & Turning 26

Guidance for coverage transitions, whether you're aging off a parent's plan, leaving a job, or bridging to Medicare.
