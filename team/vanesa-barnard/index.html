Vanesa Barnard

Advisor — Kansas, Hablo Español

Vanesa joined 316 Health Insurance in October 2024 and has quickly become an integral part of our team, leading the Spanish-speaking market. With over 20 years of experience in client relationships, Vanesa brings a wealth of knowledge, empathy, and dedication to every interaction.

Her fluency in Spanish has been instrumental in building strong connections within the Wichita Hispanic community, significantly expanding our reach and ensuring that Spanish-speaking clients receive the highest level of care and support. Vanesa connects with clients on both a cultural and linguistic level, while also driving strategic initiatives to enhance our presence and impact.

Outside of work, Vanesa is passionate about staying active, spending time with her friends and family, and volunteering at community events. Her commitment to service — both professionally and personally — makes her a trusted advisor and valued member of the 316 Health Insurance team.
